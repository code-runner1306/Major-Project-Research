Five different things

TermMeaning in this experiment
Decay, alphaA multiplier applied to the previous memory state before its delta-rule update. Closer to one means less direct decay.
TargetThe initial mean decay we request. Layer targets correspond to diagnostic half-lives 16, 32, 64 and 128 tokens.
Offset, dt_biasAn existing trainable number in the decay formula. We fit its starting value; it is not the half-life itself.
CalibrationBefore training: fit offsets on 256 training examples to match target means. No supervised loss optimization occurs in this step.
TrainingAfter calibration: optimize all learned parameters, including the offsets, to predict the correct answers.

Calibration passed means the initial target means were reached. It does not mean training improved, the targets remained fixed, or usable memory has the specified lifetime.
