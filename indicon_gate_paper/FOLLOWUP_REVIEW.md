# Follow-up revision audit

- E1 raw means are 0.9998046875 for scalar and 0.9830078125 for channel-wise, a difference of 1.6796875 percentage points. Scalar was perfect in six seeds. The other four had one error per 2,048 queries. Both designs reached lock-in on all ten seeds.
- The shared cutoff is 16 epochs on a 64-epoch cosine schedule. The scheduled rate after epoch 16 is 85.4% of peak. The paper now explicitly treats this as a limitation. The retained 36 synthetic training records show the planned seeds completed. Two outer publishing errors left completed checkpoints intact. The retained Anatomy logs show 60 completed runs with no recorded abort or replacement.
- The fresh confirmation table reports both-query, latest-value, and unrelated-retention results separately. Each overwrite seed has 512 examples and 1,024 answers. The stress comparison now has a separate three-row table.
- The extrapolation uses frozen models trained at length 64. The 128-token stress cells were evaluation-only. The new long inputs insert repeated token ID 0 immediately after the writes. Token ID 0 occurred as filler in training. The study does not isolate longer delay from length distribution shift.
- The full length sweep stores 36 aggregate results. Independent targeted replays retain per-query predictions, targets, top-two logits, margins and top-1 probabilities in NPZ files. Both reported counts reproduced exactly on repeated forwards. The label oracle found no mismatches, and all evaluated logits were finite.
- At 0/1,024 the failing checkpoint predicted 907 key-band IDs, including 289 stored keys, and 117 value-band IDs. It predicted no filler IDs. Median top-1 probability was 0.74% (only 3 of 1,024 reached 50%). These data do not support the proposed interpretation of uniformly confident wrong values.
- The SHA-256 audit matches the 12 sweep models to the fresh MQAR confirmation and to the local checkpoint bytes. The alpha probe archives 16 examples per seed for seeds 43 and 44, matched across both initializations and gate types. Its decay-only product omits updates and readout. The matched seed-43 contrast shows that the larger final-layer median product was associated with worse recall.
- The old seven-page PDF was shortened to six pages including 18 references. Public INDICON 2026 pages inspected did not state the current camera-ready limit. The supplied example is six pages, and the [preceding edition's official guidance](https://ieeeindicon.org/guidelines-and-policies/) specified six pages including references.
