# Mission: understand and defend the gating experiment

## Why
Explain what our GDN experiments change and what their results can support, so
the research design and paper claims follow from evidence rather than labels.

## Success looks like
- Distinguish decay targets, fitted offsets, training, and evaluation.
- Explain calibration success separately from improved task performance.
- Interpret the scalar/channel-wise comparison without overstating two seeds.

## Constraints
- Use plain language and concrete examples before equations.
- Ground explanations in the actual implementation and saved results.
- Time and GPU resources are limited.

## Out of scope
- Claiming demonstrated understanding before the learner has shown it.
- Unrelated terminology or a general survey of all calibration methods.
